About the workshop

Harbourline is a two-person workshop. We took over the unit in 2009 and have been making furniture in it ever since.

We work in solid timber, mostly European oak and ash, with hand-cut joints where they matter and machine work where it does not.

How a job runs

  1. You visit the workshop or we visit the room.
  2. We draw it and price it on paper.
  3. We build it, usually in six to ten weeks.
  4. We fit it.

Getting in touch

There is no contact form on this site on purpose. Call the workshop on weekday mornings, or knock on the blue door.
